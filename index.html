Archbishop Rev. Daniel Were Ohola

Spiritual Leader of Ruwe Holy Ghost Church of East Africa- West Arch Diocese

Archbishop Rev. Daniel Were Ohola serves as the spiritual head of Ruwe Holy Ghost Church. His ministry focuses on unity, empowerment, and faith renewal across all dioceses. Through his leadership, the church continues to expand its reach and strengthen its role in transforming lives through the power of the Holy Spirit.
